NDTL - Gains v2 plots the intraday levels a day trader watches, the same on any chart timeframe.

Opening range: Paper Prophet-style lock on 1-minute data. Each side follows the high or low from 9:30 and locks after 4 quiet minutes, never before 9:37.
Premarket high/low (4:00-9:30 AM) and the gap line: the 7:57 PM 3-minute candle's high on a gap up, its low on a gap down.
Prior day, week, month and quarter highs/lows, ADR and AWR bands, and the 20D/50D/200D/50W/200W SMAs.
Premarket fib: from the first premarket extreme to the second, with 0.5/0.618/0.65 levels and 1.5/1.618/1.65 extensions. Gold fill 0.5-0.618, green 0.618-0.65, mirrored on the extensions.
MTFC table: direction of the 15m, 1h, 4h, D and W candles with each one's Strat type (1/2u/2d/3), plus candle outlines and alerts when all align.

Works with extended hours on or off. "Extend lines" (on by default) keeps the OR, premarket and gap lines and the fib up until the next day replaces them; off stops them at 4:00 PM ET.
